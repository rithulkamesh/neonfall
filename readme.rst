=================
Neonfall
=================

Civilization has collapsed but its technological infrastructure continues operating. Cities have been abandoned, autonomous systems remain active, corporate facilities have become ruins, and nature has reclaimed much of the built environment.

* Exploration
* Procedural world generation
* Survival and resource management
* Construction and technological progression
* Environmental interaction
